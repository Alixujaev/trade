1. HYPOTHESIS & DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H4: Tight SIGNAL_LOW stops may be hit by intraday noise. How does a 5m ATR-scaled stop geometry
change outcomes? Pre-declared multipliers only; no variant is selected or called 'best'.
  Stop formula   : risk = ATR_5m(signal_time) * multiplier; stop = entry - risk; target = entry + 2 * risk
  ATR            : 5m ATR(14) = simple rolling mean of True Range (indicators/atr.py), continuous RTH series
  Baseline stop  : SIGNAL_LOW (DAY-02, unchanged)
  Multipliers    : 0.50, 0.75, 1.00, 1.25
  signal         : closed 5m bar T
  entry          : T + 1 5m OPEN
  target         : 2.0R
  same_bar_ambiguity: STOP_FIRST
  forced_exit    : 15:55 ET
  direction      : long-only
  costs          : 0 bps in simulation; 5/10 bps applied post-hoc to compounded return
  Population     : H4 re-simulates every baseline trade 1:1 (same setup bar, same entry bar). Baseline open-position skip decisions are frozen and not re-evaluated under H4 exit times.

11. DENOMINATORS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  win_rate                    : trades in the variant population
  profit_factor               : sum of positive gross returns / |sum of negative gross returns| (trade level)
  total_R / avg_R / median_R  : trade-level R multiples of the variant's own initial risk
  compounded_return           : product of (1 + gross_return) over trades sorted by entry_time (1 unit, no sizing)

12. INTERPRETATION RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - H4 variants do NOT share the baseline risk geometry: 1R means a different dollar/percent risk per
    variant (see section 6). Compare R-multiples together with stop distance %, not in isolation.
  - Compounded return is 1 unit per trade with no position sizing; it is not a portfolio simulation.
  - No multiplier is declared best or production-ready. Results must be read with friction (sec. 5),
    symbol concentration (sec. 8), drawdown and sample size.
  - This experiment tests stop geometry only; it does not establish that the DAY-01 signal has an edge.
  - If H4 does not help, parameters are NOT searched further.
