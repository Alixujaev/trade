11. LIMITATIONS & RESEARCH INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Factual Observations:
  1. Directional Stability: All three pre-declared thresholds (0.75R, 1.00R, 1.25R) reduce full-stop exits
     relative to baseline (from 66.49% to 54.01% - 60.97%) and improve Total R (by +38.6R to +48.9R).
  2. Magnitude Sensitivity: The BE trigger rate is highly threshold-sensitive, falling monotonically from
     32.74% at 0.75R down to 21.93% at 1.25R. Breakeven exits correspondingly fall from 1,023 down to 597.
  3. Target Cut-off Trade-off: Earlier BE activation (0.75R) cuts off more winners prematurely (304 target trades
     lost to BE), whereas later activation (1.25R) cuts off fewer winners (139 target trades lost to BE).
  4. Invariant Frictional Deficit: Compounded portfolio return remains deeply negative under 5 bps (-99.8%)
     and 10 bps (-100.0%) slippage across all three thresholds. Breakeven exits do not overcome execution drag.
  5. Research Conclusion: Stop management at 0.75R, 1.00R, and 1.25R produces directionally stable behavior,
     confirming that DAY-07 was not an artifact of the arbitrary 1.00R value. However, dynamic stop management
     alone does NOT convert the baseline VWAP Momentum strategy into a profitable edge.
