1. RESEARCH QUESTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hypothesis H6: DAY-01 VWAP Momentum signals may perform differently when the broader
US index context (SPY/QQQ) is directionally aligned with the stock setup.
This is an isolated context-filter research experiment. It does not select a 'best'
variant, does not tune parameters, and does not combine with DAY-06 or DAY-07.

2. FROZEN BASELINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following parameters and execution rules remain strictly unchanged from DAY-01:
  - Signal logic: VWAP reclaim/hold, RSI(14) > 50, RVOL(20) >= 2.0, 5m trigger (CHoCH/BOS/High)
  - 15m SMC context: Bearish structure conflict
  - Entry timing: Next 5m bar OPEN (T + 1 5m OPEN)
  - Initial stop: SIGNAL_LOW (fixed)
  - Profit target: 2.0R (fixed)
  - Ambiguity resolution: STOP_FIRST (conservative)
  - Forced exit: 15:55 ET (no overnight)
  - Direction: Long-only
  - Universe: 25 US equities (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN, META, GOOGL, TSLA, AVGO, AMD, ...)
  - Benchmark indices: SPY, QQQ
  - Study period: 2026-07-02 -> 2026-09-25 (60 RTH sessions)

3. INDEX REGIME DEFINITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dedicated module: strategy/day/index_regime.py (IndexRegimeContext, IndexRegimeDetector)
For each benchmark index (SPY and QQQ):
  - 5m VWAP relation: Latest CLOSED 5m bar available at stock signal time T.
      ABOVE_VWAP if 5m close > 5m VWAP; BELOW_VWAP if 5m close <= 5m VWAP.
  - 15m structure: Causal SMC structure from confirmed closed 15m bars (bar_end_time <= T).
      BULLISH, BEARISH, NEUTRAL, or UNKNOWN (if < 5 closed 15m bars).
  - Bullish index context: 5m close > 5m VWAP AND 15m structure is BULLISH.
      Otherwise: Non-bullish (or UNKNOWN if data insufficient).
  - Aligned bullish: Both SPY AND QQQ are bullish.
  - Aligned bearish: Both SPY AND QQQ have 5m close < VWAP AND 15m structure bearish.
  - Mixed: Sufficient data, neither aligned bullish nor aligned bearish.
  - Unknown: Missing / insufficient index data (<1 5m bar or <5 15m bars).

4. VARIANT DEFINITIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Baseline: No index filter. All DAY-01 signals executed.
  H6-A    : SPY bullish confluence. Only evaluate signals where SPY is bullish.
  H6-B    : QQQ bullish confluence. Only evaluate signals where QQQ is bullish.
  H6-C    : SPY AND QQQ bullish confluence. Only evaluate signals where both are bullish.

15. METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Limited sample: 60 RTH sessions (July 2, 2026 - September 25, 2026).
  2. Universe constraint: 25 mega/large-cap equities; may not generalize to small/mid caps.
  3. Single market regime: Historical period contains specific prevailing macroeconomic dynamics.
  4. No out-of-sample period: Findings reflect historical context only, not forward predictive power.
  5. Execution friction: Deeply negative performance under 5 bps and 10 bps slippage.
  6. News/catalysts unavailable: Fundamental catalyst data is not integrated.

16. FACTUAL FINDINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Signal reduction: Requiring SPY bullish confluence filters out 59.97% of signals (2,278 trades);
     QQQ bullish confluence filters out 57.88% (2,397 trades); both SPY and QQQ bullish filters out
     68.02% of signals (1,820 trades).
  2. Performance under index confluence:
     - Baseline (no filter)   : 5691 trades, WR=30.42%, PF=0.9388, Total R=-738.98R, Gross Return=-41.19%
     - H6-A (SPY bullish)     : 2254 trades, WR=30.88%, PF=0.9686, Total R=-278.85R, Gross Return=-10.99%
     - H6-B (QQQ bullish)     : 2304 trades, WR=30.34%, PF=0.9295, Total R=-302.69R, Gross Return=-20.82%
     - H6-C (SPY+QQQ bullish) : 1833 trades, WR=29.62%, PF=0.9284, Total R=-288.37R, Gross Return=-16.83%
  3. Total R impact:
     - Baseline: -738.98R across 5691 trades (Avg R = -0.1299R)
     - H6-C:     -288.37R across 1833 trades (Avg R = -0.1573R)
     Filtering to aligned bullish regime removes -450.61R of losses,
     though the average R per trade remains negative in all variants.
  4. Opposed regime (aligned bearish): Signals generated when both indices are bearish
     produced 1178 trades with WR=29.46%, PF=1.0024, Total R=-184.11R.
  5. Execution friction: All variants collapse into severe negative portfolio returns under realistic
     5 bps (-86.70% to -99.80%) and 10 bps (-97.87% to -100.00%) slippage.
  6. Churn remains elevated: Even in H6-C, symbol-sessions with >=2 trades account for
     61.61% of traded sessions, and 29.53% have >=5 trades.

17. NEXT RESEARCH QUESTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While index confluence reduces raw trade count and total losses by cutting trades in unfavorable
regimes, it does not convert the DAY-01 VWAP Momentum strategy into a positive expectancy edge
under realistic friction.
Subsequent research must explore H3 (trade frequency / churn limits) and catalyst integration
independently, without premature parameter tuning or deploying unvalidated combinations.
